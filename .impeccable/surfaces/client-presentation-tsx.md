# Off the Record: room, phone and host surfaces

Scope: the whole client (home, player phone, projector presentation, host studio and live room). Mode: Experience for the projector and phones; the host studio is Operate inside the same world.

Audience and job: colleagues at a dim after-work office party guess who each song is really about; the host prepares and runs it from a laptop. Constraints from PRODUCT.md hold: no answer leaks, host never sees choices, projector legible from the back.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The song wears one colleague's name tag; the room finds whose it really is. Refuses the dark game-show stage with one neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Lanyard-navy ground (#17234a) owns every viewport. Objects are office-party ephemera: red HELLO my-name-is stickers with marker-written names, perforated raffle tickets in yellow/pink/teal carrying codes and numbers, name badges with coloured top bands. Bricolage Grotesque for printed parts, Permanent Marker only for names. No boxed cards on room surfaces; stickers and tickets are the only objects.

STORY: Players see the decoy idea instantly (a tag that lies), fill in their own sticker to join, pick a suspect badge, and watch the real name tag slap onto the photo at the reveal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Projector lobby: giant tilted HELLO sticker reading "Off the Record" left, yellow raffle ticket right with QR and room code as its serial, joined players' badges sticking along the bottom. Phone join: the nickname field is the white strip of a HELLO sticker; red Join button under it.

FORM: The Name Tag, position 7 of the grounded list, seed: a1bb455c5285f746. Signature interaction: the sticker: picking lifts a badge, locking sticks it down, the reveal slaps the true name tag on the photo. Motion: one slap-on settle (scale/rotate/shadow, exponential ease-out), reduced-motion static.
